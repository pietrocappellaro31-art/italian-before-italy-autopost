Ask the price in Italian, 3 ways. Voiced by Pietro, a native speaker.

Quanto costa? = How much does it cost?
Quanto costa questo? = How much is this?
Quant'è? = How much is it? (the total)

Say each one out loud after Pietro, then save this for your trip.
