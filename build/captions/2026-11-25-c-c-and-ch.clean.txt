C or CH in Italian? One simple rule tells you how every C sounds.

Soft C, like CH in chat: CE, CI
cena = dinner
ciao = hi / bye
Ci vediamo domani. = See you tomorrow.

Hard C, like K in kit: CHE, CHI, and C before A, O, U
Che cosa? = What?
chiesa = church
casa = house
come = how
cuore = heart
