How to ask the price in Italian: one item, this one, or the total.

Quanto costa? = How much does it cost?
Quanto costa questo? = How much is this?
Quanto costa quello? = How much is that?
Quanto costano? = How much do they cost?
Quant'è? = How much is it? (the total)
