Ordering coffee in Italy? Here's the polite way to ask, said by a native speaker.

Vorrei un caffè, per favore. = I'd like a coffee, please.
Un caffè is an espresso. Vorrei (I'd like) sounds polite and works anywhere.

Un cappuccino e un cornetto, per favore. = A cappuccino and a croissant, please.

Listen, say it out loud in the pause, then try the quiz. Save it for your trip.
