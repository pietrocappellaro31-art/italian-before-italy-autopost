How are you? in Italian, 3 ways plus a polite reply. Voiced by Pietro, a native speaker.

Come sta? = How are you? (formal, Lei)
Come stai? = How are you? (informal, tu)
Come va? = How's it going?
Bene, grazie. E lei? = Fine, thanks. And you? (formal)
