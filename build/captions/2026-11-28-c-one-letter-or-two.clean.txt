One letter or two? In Italian a double consonant changes the word: sete is thirst, sette is seven.

sete = thirst
sette = seven
pala = shovel
palla = ball
caro = dear / expensive
carro = cart
Sono le sette. = It's seven o'clock.
Ho sete. = I'm thirsty.
