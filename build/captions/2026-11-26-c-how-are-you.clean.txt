How are you? in Italian: formal, informal and everyday.

Come sta? = How are you? (formal, Lei)
Come stai? = How are you? (informal, tu)
Come va? = How's it going?

How to answer:
Bene, grazie. = Fine, thanks.
Molto bene! = Very well!
Così così. = So-so.
E lei? = And you? (formal)
E tu? = And you? (informal)
