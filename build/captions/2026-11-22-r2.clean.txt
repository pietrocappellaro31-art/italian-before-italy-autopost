pala or palla? In Italian, one letter or two changes the word. Listen to a native speaker.

pala = shovel · palla = ball
caro = dear, expensive · carro = cart
sete = thirst · sette = seven

Hold the double consonant a little longer:
Ho sete. = I'm thirsty
Sono le sette. = It's seven o'clock
