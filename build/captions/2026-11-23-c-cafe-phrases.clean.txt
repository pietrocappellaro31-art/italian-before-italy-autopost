5 Italian phrases for your first café, from ordering to paying.

Vorrei un caffè, per favore. = I'd like a coffee, please.
Al banco, grazie. = At the counter, thanks.
Quanto costa? = How much does it cost?
Quant'è? = How much is it? (the total)
Il conto, per favore. = The bill, please.
