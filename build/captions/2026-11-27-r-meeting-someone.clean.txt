Meet someone new in Italian, in 4 lines. Both parts voiced by Pietro, a native speaker.

Ciao! Io sono Luca. = Hi! I'm Luca.
Piacere, Marco. Di dove sei? = Nice to meet you, I'm Marco. Where are you from?
Sono di Milano. E tu? = I'm from Milan. And you?
Sono americano, ma studio qui. = I'm American, but I study here.

Sei and tu are the informal you, as between friends.
