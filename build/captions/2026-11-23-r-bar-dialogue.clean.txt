Your first Italian bar, in 6 lines. Both parts voiced by Pietro, a native speaker.

Buongiorno, cosa prende? = Good morning, what will you have?
Un caffè e un cornetto, per favore. = A coffee and a croissant, please.
Al banco o al tavolo? = At the counter or at a table?
Al banco, grazie. = At the counter, thanks.
Ecco a lei. = Here you are.
Grazie, buona giornata! = Thanks, have a nice day!

In many Italian bars you order and drink standing at the counter (al banco).
